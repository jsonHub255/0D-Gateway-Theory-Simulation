\begin{abstract}
We evaluate quantum tunneling transmission probabilities across a 3D potential barrier with a localized 4D spatial interaction constrained to the origin $(0,0,0)$. Using Bethe-Peierls boundary conditions, we regularize zero-range interactions to ensure grid-scale independence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The hypothesis posits a spatial intersection of a higher-dimensional coordinate $w$ at the zero-dimensional origin point $(0,0,0)$ in 3D Euclidean space.

\section{Renormalization Group (RG) Flow \& Grid Invariance}
To ensure the physical validity of the origin coupling, the bare coupling constant $g(\sigma)$ must be renormalized as the spatial grid resolution parameter $\sigma \to 0$. We express the regularized potential $V_{\text{reg}}(\mathbf{r})$ using a normalized Gaussian envelope:

\begin{equation}
V_{\text{reg}}(\mathbf{r}) = -g(\sigma) \left(2\pi \sigma^2\right)^{-3/2} \exp\left(-\frac{r^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)
\end{equation}

Matching $V_{\text{reg}}(\mathbf{r})$ to the low-energy s-wave scattering amplitude determined by the Bethe-Peierls boundary condition $\psi(r) \sim C(1/r - 1/a_s)$ yields the explicit RG equation for the bare coupling:

\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{g(\sigma)} = \frac{m}{4\pi \hbar^2 a_s} + \frac{m}{4\pi \hbar^2 \sigma \sqrt{2\pi}}
\end{equation}

Solving for $g(\sigma)$ gives the continuous scaling relation:

\begin{equation}
g(\sigma) = \frac{4\pi \hbar^2}{m} \left( \frac{1}{a_s} - \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} \right)^{-1}
\end{equation}

As $\sigma \to 0$, $g(\sigma)$ naturally flows to absorb the $1/\sigma$ divergence, holding the physical s-wave scattering length $a_s$ constant and rendering observable transmission probabilities invariant under grid refinement.
